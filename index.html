Quick summary

  • Who we are: Collection (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the Collection mobile application and related services at collection.app.
  • What we collect: Account information you provide at signup (name, email, date of birth, username, country), profile data you add (bio, avatar), your rankings, reviews, comments, social connections, and usage data necessary to operate the service.
  • Third-party services: We use Google Firebase for authentication, storage, and backend infrastructure. Movie and TV metadata, images, and streaming availability are provided through The Movie Database (TMDB) API. Collection is not endorsed, certified, or otherwise approved by TMDB.
  • Your controls: You can set your account to private, block users, report content, change your password, and permanently delete your account from within the app (Settings → Delete account).
  • Age requirement: You must be at least 13 years old to create an account.

TMDB attribution

This application uses TMDB and the TMDB APIs but is not endorsed, certified, or otherwise approved by TMDB.

Cached title metadata from TMDB is automatically refreshed and is never retained for longer than six months, in line with the TMDB Terms of Use.
