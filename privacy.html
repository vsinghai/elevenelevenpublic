Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 9, 2026 · Last updated: June 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Collection (“Collection,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you use the Collection mobile application and related services (collectively, the “Service”).

By creating an account or using the Service, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, do not use the Service.

1. Who we are

Collection is a social platform for discovering, ranking, reviewing, and sharing opinions about movies and television. The Service includes features such as personalized rankings, a social feed, follow relationships, comments and likes, title search, trending discovery, and streaming availability information.

Contact: For privacy-related questions or requests, contact us at privacy@collection.app.

2. Eligibility and children’s privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13. You must be at least 13 years of age to register. During signup we collect your date of birth and verify that you meet this minimum age requirement.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected such information, we will take steps to delete the account and associated data. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us information, contact privacy@collection.app.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Information you provide directly

  • Account registration: First name, last name, email address, date of birth, username, password, and country.
  • Terms acceptance: A record that you accepted our Terms of Service and this Privacy Policy at signup (accepted_terms).
  • Profile information: Bio, profile avatar (optional, uploaded by you), and account visibility preference (public or private).
  • User-generated content (UGC): Rankings and reviews (numeric scores from 0–10 and review text up to 2,000 characters), comments on posts (up to 1,000 characters), likes, and any optional details you include in content reports.
  • Social activity: Accounts you follow, follow requests you send or receive (for private accounts), users you block, and notifications you receive.
  • Media library data: Titles you save, mark as watched or want-to-watch, scores you assign, ranking positions, pairwise comparison history, and ranking-engine metadata associated with your lists.
  • Support and moderation: Content reports you file (including report reason and optional details) and correspondence if you contact us.

3.2 Information collected automatically

  • Authentication identifiers: A unique user ID (UID) assigned by Firebase Authentication when you create an account.
  • Device and app data: Information needed to operate the app, such as device type, operating system, and app version. Firebase App Check may collect attestation signals in production builds to help protect backend services from abuse.
  • Usage and interaction data: Timestamps of account creation and updates, post/comment/like activity, follow graph changes, and notification delivery and read status.
  • Local caching: The app enables Firestore offline persistence and may cache data locally on your device (including search results and media metadata) to improve performance and reduce network usage.

3.3 Information we do not collect

  • We do not store your password in our database. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication.
  • We do not currently integrate third-party advertising networks or sell your personal information.
  • We do not receive your precise GPS location. Country is collected at signup as a profile field you select.

4. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account and authenticate you.
  • Provide core features: rankings, reviews, feed, search, trending, title details, episode lists, and streaming availability.
  • Operate social features: following, private-account follow requests, follower/following lists, and user search.
  • Deliver in-app notifications (e.g., new followers, likes, comments).
  • Enforce account visibility rules so private libraries are visible only to approved followers.
  • Process content reports and user blocks to maintain community safety.
  • Comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.
  • Protect the security and integrity of the Service, including abuse prevention via Firebase App Check (when enabled).
  • Improve and maintain the Service.

5. How we share your information

5.1 With other users (social features)

Depending on your privacy settings and social relationships:

  • Public accounts: Your profile (username, name, bio, avatar, follower/following counts, taste tags), posts, reviews, and rankings are visible to other signed-in Collection users. Anyone can send you a follow request or follow you directly.
  • Private accounts: Your profile is visible, but your media library (saved, watched, want-to-watch, scores, and rankings) is visible only to users you have approved as followers. Other users may send follow requests that you can accept or deny.
  • Feed and interactions: Posts, comments, and likes you create are visible to other signed-in users according to normal social visibility (e.g., posts appear in followers’ feeds).
  • Blocking: If you block a user, we store that block on your account. Blocked users are unfollowed and should no longer see your content in their experience.

5.2 With service providers

We use third-party infrastructure providers to operate the Service:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Cloud Storage, App Check) — account management, data storage, backend processing, avatar hosting, and security.
  • The Movie Database (TMDB) — movie and TV metadata, images, search, trending lists, season/episode data, and watch-provider (streaming) availability. See Section 7 for details.

These providers process data on our behalf under their own terms and privacy policies. Firebase services are governed by Google’s Firebase Terms and Google’s Privacy Policy.

7. TMDB API — data sources and attribution

This application uses TMDB and the TMDB APIs but is not endorsed, certified, or otherwise approved by TMDB.

Collection displays movie and television information sourced from The Movie Database (TMDB). TMDB data is accessed exclusively through our secure backend (Firebase Cloud Functions); the TMDB API key is never exposed to the client application.

7.1 What TMDB data we fetch and display

  • Title metadata: names, release years, overviews/synopses, genres, cast, posters, backdrops, and related details.
  • Search results when you search for movies and TV shows.
  • Trending and discovery lists.
  • TV season and episode information for episode-level ranking.
  • Watch-provider (streaming/rent/buy) availability, including provider names and logos.
  • Images served from TMDB’s image CDN (image.tmdb.org).

7.2 How TMDB data is cached

  • Title metadata cached on our servers is refreshed if older than 30 days.
  • Watch-provider data is re-validated on a similar schedule; titles with no providers are re-checked more frequently (approximately every 7 days).
  • In compliance with TMDB’s Terms of Use, cached TMDB payloads are automatically purged if older than approximately five months and are never retained longer than six months.
  • Search results are ephemeral on the server (not written to our TMDB cache) and may be cached locally on your device.

7.3 What TMDB receives

When our backend requests data from TMDB, TMDB’s servers receive the API request (including query parameters such as title IDs or search terms). TMDB does not receive your Collection account credentials. TMDB’s handling of request data is governed by TMDB’s Privacy Policy and TMDB’s Terms of Use.

7.4 Your ratings vs. TMDB ratings

Scores and reviews you publish in Collection are your own user-generated content. They are separate from TMDB’s community vote averages. Collection may compute aggregate ratings across Collection users for display within the app; these aggregates are not submitted to TMDB.

8. Data storage, retention, and security

8.1 Where data is stored

Your data is stored in Google Cloud infrastructure, primarily in the us-central1 region, including:

  • Cloud Firestore: User profiles, posts, comments, likes, follow graph, media state, ranking data, notifications, content reports, and TMDB metadata cache.
  • Firebase Authentication: Email and authentication credentials.
  • Firebase Storage: Profile avatars (avatars/{uid}.jpg) and a public trending feed JSON file.
  • Your device: Local secure storage for session state and Firestore offline cache.

8.2 Retention

  • Account data: Retained while your account is active.
  • Deleted accounts: When you delete your account (Settings → Delete account), we permanently delete your Firebase Auth identity, user profile and subcollections (media state, followers, following, notifications, follow requests, ranking metadata, comparisons), your posts, and your username reservation. Deletion is intended to be irreversible.
  • Content reports: May be retained after account deletion where needed for safety, legal, or abuse-prevention purposes.
  • TMDB cache: Purged per the schedule in Section 7.2.

8.3 Security measures

  • Authentication is required for all Firestore reads and writes; anonymous access is not permitted.
  • Security rules restrict clients to modifying only their own data; server-maintained fields (like counts) cannot be forged by clients.
  • TMDB API keys are stored in server-side secret management, not in the app.
  • Firebase App Check helps verify that requests originate from the genuine app (when enforcement is enabled).
  • Passwords are managed by Firebase Authentication and are not stored in plaintext in our database.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.
